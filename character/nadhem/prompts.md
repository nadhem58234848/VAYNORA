# برومبت شخصية ناظم الملثّمة (ChatGPT / Gemini)

**كيفاش:** ارفع صورك الـ4 (قدّام، جنب، جنب ثاني، ظهر) + صورتين الكومبريشن، وبعد انسخ البرومبت.

## 1) ورقة الشخصية (قدّام، جنبين، خلف)

```
Using the attached photos of me as the identity reference (same short curly black hair with a short fringe, same short black beard on the jaw and chin, same skin tone and head shape) and the attached compression shirt images as the clothing reference, create a CHARACTER TURNAROUND SHEET: four full-body views side by side on plain off-white textured paper — front view, left side profile, right side profile, back view. Same character in every view.

Body: much more muscular than in the photos — athletic bodybuilder physique, broad shoulders, big chest and arms, clear V-taper, flat stomach.

Outfit: the exact black long-sleeve VAYNORA compression shirt from the reference (black fabric, thin red lines on shoulders, arms and sides, small red flame emblem on the chest, the word VAYNORA in red written vertically down the spine on the back), black jogger pants, black sneakers.

Face: he wears a matte black half balaclava that covers the nose, mouth and chin, with a small red flame emblem on the front. The eyes area is heavily blurred/out of focus: no visible eyes and no visible facial features at all. The character must be completely faceless.

Style: semi-realistic charcoal and graphite pencil illustration, muted black/grey/beige palette, red only on the shirt details and the mask emblem, rough sketchy grey strokes behind the figure, soft cinematic light. No other text, no labels, no watermark.
```

## 2) لوحة بوست (بعد ما توافق على الشخصية)

```
Use the character from the turnaround sheet (same body, hair, beard, mask, VAYNORA compression shirt, blurred eyes, completely faceless). Create one wide 16:9 panel in the same charcoal/graphite pencil style on off-white textured paper: [SCENE, e.g. he stands under a cold shower, arms crossed, water drops on his shoulders]. Place the character on the LEFT half; keep the RIGHT half empty light paper for an Arabic title I will add later. No text in the image.
```

**ملاحظة:** إذا طلّع الوجه أو العينين واضحين، قلّو: «Blur the eyes more, the face must be fully hidden, no facial features».
