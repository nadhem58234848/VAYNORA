# Storytelling (القصة اللي تشد)

## The source: only the truth
- Nadhem's story (from project memory): drugs, rap and no prayer at 17; prison 11 Apr 2023 to 20 Mar 2024 (Sousse, Mahdia; cell, scabies); relapse after release + heartbreak; tawba moment "يا ربي راني تعبت" after a prayer video; rebuilt with prayer, sport, reading; trade from zero (aunt's shop unpaid, street stall, home sales); VAYNORA 80 pieces failed (~2000 TND loss), rented shop closed, debts mostly repaid; led Fajr and became a volunteer imam for a time (NOT an imam now); seclusion period in another city; IG group of 100+ learners removed after a relapse.
- NEVER invent, exaggerate or merge events. Stories die on generic lines; they live on specific details. If a scene lacks details, ASK Nadhem 3-5 precise questions before writing: What did you see/hear? What exact sentence was said? What did you do with your hands? What time was it? What did you feel in your body?
- Other people's stories (followers, buyers) only with permission and without names.

## Core principles
1. **Start in the middle of the scene** (in medias res), never with background. "الساعة 3 متاع الليل، في بيت فيها 20 واحد، والجرب ماكلني." Context comes later in one line.
2. **"But / therefore", not "and then".** Each beat must cause the next: "كنت نحسب روحي تبدلت… أما نهار خرجت…" / "…ولهذا قررت…". A list of events is not a story.
3. **One story = one change.** Before (what he believed/was) → event that breaks it → after (what he learned/did). Name the change in one sentence before writing.
4. **Specific > general.** Numbers, places, objects, exact words: "80 قطعة"، "2000 دينار"، "سجن المهدية"، "يا ربي راني تعبت". One true detail beats ten adjectives.
5. **Show the low point honestly.** The relapse, the shame, the loss. Vulnerability builds trust; but no glorifying sin and no graphic detail. The goal is hope, not shock.
6. **Dialogue and inner voice.** Put real sentences in quotes ("قالي الحارس…", "قلت في روحي…"). Inner voice in darija makes the viewer hear himself.
7. **The viewer is the hero, Nadhem is the guide.** End every story by turning it to him: "وانت زادة، كان…". The lesson must be something he can apply today (prayer, a routine step, the challenge, the book).
8. **Open loops.** Tease the outcome early ("والحاجة اللي صارت بعد هاذي بدلتلي حياتي") and pay it off at the end. In series, end each episode on the next question.
9. **Emotion through contrast:** prison cell vs leading Fajr; 0 TND vs his own brand; the rapper vs the man reciting Quran. Contrast is the brand's core: «اصنع من الألم قوة».
10. **Faith thread, never preachy:** the turning points credit Allah (tawba, du'a), with an authentic verse/hadith only when it fits and is cited.

## Story structures to pick from
- **Mini story (30-45s reel):** Hook scene (0-3s) → conflict (what was at stake) → turn (the moment it changed) → lesson for him → CTA.
- **Series episode (60-90s):** Recap in 1 line ("في الحلقة اللي فاتت…") → new scene → rising tension → cliffhanger question. Title format: «حلقة X: …».
- **Origin story (sales page / ad / pinned reel):** Who I was → the bottom → the discovery (what worked) → the proof (where I am now) → why I made this (book/challenge) → invitation.
- **Lesson-from-a-moment:** a small daily scene (missed Fajr, gym, a customer) → the realization → the rule.
- **Follower story (with permission):** his question/message → Nadhem's answer from his own experience → the step.

## Where it is used
- Reels: pair with viral-reel-scripts (hook + table format) and retention-editing-ad-creatives (B-roll per beat, captions).
- Ads: the origin story is the "proof" beat; keep it 10-15s and first-person (no personal-attribute claims about the viewer).
- Carousels: one scene per slide, last slide = lesson + CTA.
- DM sales: one-line personal proof ("أنا زادة عشتها سنين") from whatsapp-dm-sales-closing.
- Future big book «حرب النفس»: same rules, longer scenes.

## Output format
1. The change in one sentence (before → after).
2. Questions for Nadhem if details are missing (stop and ask before writing the full story).
3. 3 hook scenes to choose from.
4. The story as a table: time | spoken (darija) | on-screen text | shot / B-roll / re-enactment idea.
5. The ending turned to the viewer + one CTA.
6. Check: every fact traceable to what Nadhem told us; no cliché lines ("بدايتك موش نهايتك" style); would Nadhem say each line out loud?
