# Personal brand + content system (البرسونال براند وصناعة المحتوى)

## Positioning (decided by Nadhem, don't drift)
- One line: **"ناظم، نبني روتيني وديني ونفسي قدامكم، ونعاونكم تبنيو روتينكم."**
- Nadhem is not a perfect teacher; he is a man walking the road in public (prison → tawba → rebuilding). He helps people build a routine, organize prayer and quit addictions. Message: the past does not have to end the future. Umbrella: «حرب النفس» / «اصنع من الألم قوة».
- Every reel features Nadhem (face, story, or opinion). Generic viral faith reels bring views, not followers: use them rarely, never as the backbone.
- Never write in a way that claims he is an imam now, a sheikh or a scholar. Religious content = authentic sources only (salaf/athari stance).
- North star: the VAYNORA training hall (training, recovery, discipline, Quran, aqeeda). Each piece of content is a brick toward it.

## Content pillars (weekly mix, 7 reels)
1. **قصتي (Story)** 2/week: series episodes from his real life (use story-telling-harb).
2. **المعركة (The fight / addiction)** 2/week: the buyer's questions (العميل-المثالي.md), triggers, relapse, hope. Feeds the book.
3. **الروتين (Routine & discipline)** 2/week: his real day: Fajr, Quran memorization, workout, MMA, work, sleep. "Build with me" format. Feeds the challenge/app.
4. **الدين (Faith foundation)** 1/week: prayer, tawba, a short authentic reminder, told through his experience.
Avatar split: ~5 for كريم (ambitious outer life, hidden struggle), ~2 for الياس (pain, "ماعادش عايش").

## Formats that build a personal brand
- Series with numbers ("حلقة 3 من قصتي", "اليوم 12 من حرب النفس") → people come back.
- Build-in-public: show the real day, including bad days (no glorified sin, honest about falling).
- Q&A from followers' real questions (with permission, no names).
- Opinion / contrarian takes in his voice.
- Checklist/challenge IMAGE posts as collab (nadhem_elaashek + vaynora_): "هل أنت مستعد لهذا التحدي؟" did 338K views and 1,177 shares on 30 Sep 2026. Repeat this format.
- Behind the scenes of VAYNORA (shirt samples, the book, the app).
Stories daily (low effort): a tip from the book + poll/question box + "الرابط في البايو". Stories build trust with existing followers; reels bring new people.

## The ladder (viewer → buyer)
Reel (free value) → follow → story / comment keyword «حرب» → free 21-day challenge (Telegram group, starts 1 Nov 2026) → book «معركة الخلوات» (30 TND) → paid 90-day program → future book «حرب النفس» → VAYNORA clothing → the hall.
Every week: at least 1 piece pushes the free challenge, 1 soft book mention. Never sell in every reel; the CTA goes at the END only.

## Production system (phone only, zero budget)
- 1 reel/day. Batch-film 2 days/week in the Kendar studio (3-4 reels per session). Write scripts first (viral-reel-scripts), edit in CapCut or with Claude («منتج»).
- Weekly planning ritual (e.g. Saturday): pick 7 topics from the pillars + last week's winners (weekly-content-analytics), write the scripts, plan the shoot.
- Keep an ideas bank: every DM question, comment and lesson he lives = a future reel.
- Don't post 4-5 times a day (burnout and falling reach happened in Sept 2026). Consistency > volume.
