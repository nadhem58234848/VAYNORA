# Weekly numbers review (تحليل الأرقام كل أسبوع)

## Data sources
- Instagram accounts nadhem_elaashek and vaynora_ via the Windsor_ai MCP (connector slug `instagram`; use get_connectors, get_fields, get_data; trial plan, avoid force_refresh). If Windsor is unavailable, ask Nadhem for screenshots of IG Insights (Overview + top reels).
- Sales: Nadhem's phone note / WhatsApp labels (keyword per ad), Lemon Squeezy orders if active, Meta Ads Manager numbers if ads ran.
- Challenge «حرب النفس»: participants sheet (see vay-system/memory/harb-tracking-setup.md).
- Previous weeks: vay-system/analytics/ (create weekly-YYYY-MM-DD.md there; read last week first to compare).

## What to measure (per reel, last 7 days)
- Views, reach, % non-followers reached.
- Avg watch time and watch time ÷ length (retention). Skip rate if available.
- Sends (shares) per reach, saves per reach, likes per reach, comments.
- Follows gained from the reel.
- Hook type and avatar (كريم / الياس) and topic, tagged from the script/caption.
Account level: followers start → end, posts count, profile visits, link clicks, DMs/leads, sales, ad spend and cost per sale.

## How to judge
- Rank reels by sends per reach and retention, not raw views. A reel with fewer views but high sends/saves is a better ad and part-2 candidate.
- Views but no follows = generic viral content (wrong for the personal brand). Follows + DMs = the story/solution content we want.
- Compare hook types and avatars across weeks; double down on the winner, drop the loser after 2 weeks of evidence.
- Note posting consistency (target 1 reel/day) and burnout signs (many posts, falling reach). Quality > volume.
- Separate signal from noise: one week, one reel is a hint, not a rule. Say so.

## Output (short, darija, phone-readable)
1. The week in 3 numbers (followers +X, best reel, sales/leads).
2. Top 3 reels and WHY they worked (hook, topic, retention).
3. Worst reel and the likely reason.
4. Proven reel to boost / use as ad (hand off to media buying).
5. The 3 actions for next week (e.g. part 2 of X, more كريم contrarian hooks, fix first 3s).
