# Viral reel scripts + hooks (سكريبت + هوك)

## Context you must respect
- Creator: ناظم (Nadhem), account nadhem_elaashek. Umbrella brand «حرب النفس», slogan «اصنع من الألم قوة». Clothing brand VAYNORA.
- Positioning: Nadhem's REAL journey (prison, relapse, tawba, rebuilding with prayer/sport/reading, trade from zero). Every reel shows Nadhem's face, story or opinion. Never invent or exaggerate story events; if a detail is missing, ASK Nadhem for it (place, exact sentence he said, who was there).
- Aqeeda: salaf/athari. Any hadith or verse must be authentic and cited (book + number). If unsure, don't use it.
- Avatars: **كريم** (outer, ambitious, gym/work, porn secretly kills his discipline; ~5 reels/week) and **الياس** (inner, pain-driven, feels "ماعادش عايش"; ~2/week). Pick ONE avatar per script and write in his words. Real buyer questions in vay-system/ads/العميل-المثالي.md are ready-made topics.
- Audience: 63% men, biggest 18-34, 69% Tunisia. Language: Tunisian darija, short spoken sentences, no فصحى lecture tone (verses/hadith stay in فصحى).
- Nadhem rejected generic scripts ("بدايتك موش نهايتك" type lines). Concrete scene > abstract wisdom. Banned: clichés, "في هذا الفيديو سأتحدث عن", greetings at the start, "تابعني" before giving value.

## What the algorithm rewards (2025-2026)
Instagram ranks Reels mainly on watch time / completion, sends per reach (DM shares, strongest for reaching non-followers), and likes per reach. A small non-follower test group decides if it spreads. So: win the first 1-2 seconds, keep them to the end, and give them a reason to SEND it to a friend ("ابعثها لصاحبك اللي...").

## Hook rules (first 1-3 seconds)
A hook = spoken line + on-screen text + visual, all saying the same thing. Max ~12 words spoken. Write 5 hooks per script, label the type, recommend one.
Hook types that fit the brand:
1. **Pain mirror**: describe his exact private moment. "تسكّر الباب، تحل التليفون، وبعد عشر دقايق تكره روحك."
2. **Confession / story open loop**: "نهار دخلت الحبس كان عمري 19 سنة… والحاجة اللي كسرتني ماكانتش الحبس."
3. **Contrarian**: "الزواج موش باش يداويك من العادة. وهاذي علاش."
4. **Cost / consequence**: "5 حاجات تخسرهم كل مرة تطيح ومافيبالكش."
5. **Specific number / timeline**: "66 يوم. هاذا الوقت اللي يلزمك باش…"
6. **Question he already asks himself**: "علاش ديما تطيح في الليل بالذات؟"
7. **Callout**: "كان تصلي وترجع للعادة، هالفيديو ليك."
8. **Pattern interrupt visual**: start mid-action (wudu water, punching bag, walking out of a dark room) with text on screen.
Hook checklist: Is it about HIM (not about Nadhem)? Is there a gap he needs closed? Can it be understood muted (text on screen)? Would كريم stop scrolling in the gym locker room?

## Script structure (30-60s; story episodes up to 90s)
1. **Hook** (0-3s) one of the above.
2. **Stakes / re-hook** (3-8s): why it matters now, or "والحاجة الثالثة هي اللي ماحد يحكي عليها".
3. **Body** (one idea only): story beat OR 3 points max. Each line 3-8 words. Open a small loop every ~8-10s ("وهنا صارت الحاجة اللي بدلت كل شي…").
4. **Payoff**: the insight / the turn / the practical step he can do today.
5. **CTA** (one only), matched to the goal:
   - reach: "ابعثها لصاحبك اللي يلزمو يسمعها"
   - follow: "تبعني، كل نهار نحكيلك كيفاش نبني روتيني"
   - lead: "اكتب «حرب» في الكومنتار ونبعثلك…" / challenge / book «معركة الخلوات»
Loop ending: when possible, make the last line flow back into the first so rewatches count.

## Output format
For every script give:
- Avatar + goal (reach / follow / lead) + length.
- 5 hooks (type label), recommended one marked.
- Script as a table: time | spoken (darija) | on-screen text (2-4 words, keyword to color red) | shot/B-roll.
- Caption: first line = hook restated + CTA; 2-4 short lines; 3-5 hashtags max (Arabic + #حرب_النفس).
- Cover text (≤5 words).
- Filming note: location (Kendar studio / outside / prayer mat), energy, one take or cuts.

## Series & volume
1 reel/day, batch-film 2 days/week. Use formats that can repeat: "حلقة X من قصتي", "سؤال من متابع", "غلطة طيحتني", "روتيني اليوم", "5 حاجات…". Track which hook type wins (sends per reach, avg watch time) and write more of the winner.

## Before delivering, check
- Every line passes "would Nadhem actually say this out loud in darija?"
- No invented events, no unsourced hadith, no shaming tone (hope > fear; mention doctor, never fear-monger about diseases).
- Pornography topic: speak of it as "العادة / الإباحية / الإدمان" plainly but never graphic.
