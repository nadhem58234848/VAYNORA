# Small-budget media buying (ميديا باينغ بميزانية صغيرة)

## Context
- Products: book «معركة الخلوات» (30 TND, PDF sent on WhatsApp +216 58 886 475; abroad via Lemon Squeezy), free 21-day «حرب النفس» challenge (Telegram group), later 90-day program, VAYNORA compression shirt pre-order.
- Money: Nadhem is broke. Tests are 5-10 TND per creative. Payment via a virtual card funded with USDT (Bybit card / RedotPay). Never buy ad accounts or pay middlemen. Use Meta Ads Manager in the mobile BROWSER, not the iPhone app boost (Apple adds ~40% fee).
- Lessons history: vay-system/memory/media-buying-course.md. Continue from there, don't repeat lessons.
- Teach in short Tunisian darija steps, one screen at a time, phone-only.

## Core principles (2026)
1. **Organic first, then pay.** Only promote a creative that already proved itself organically (sends/saves per reach, avg watch time). Money amplifies winners.
2. **Creative is the targeting.** Meta's Andromeda system finds people from the creative. Go broad (country + 18+ age floor, no interests) and test different ANGLES/HOOKS, not audiences.
3. **Consolidate.** One campaign, one ad set, 3-5 different ads. Tiny budgets split across many ad sets never learn.
4. **Don't touch it daily.** Let an ad run 48-72h before judging (unless it's clearly broken). Big changes reset learning.
5. **Conversations objective** fits Tunisia: buyer messages on WhatsApp/IG, Nadhem closes in DM. Never message cold numbers in bulk (WhatsApp got restricted once); buyers message first.

## Setup recipe (Ads Manager, mobile browser)
- Campaign: Engagement → Messaging (WhatsApp or Instagram DM). Advantage campaign budget OFF for tests, or one ad set anyway.
- Ad set: Tunisia (+ Algeria/Morocco only for Lemon Squeezy offers), age 18-40 min floor 18, gender all (Advantage+ audience on), placements Advantage+ (or IG Reels + FB Reels if budget tiny). Budget ~$2-3/day.
- Ads: 3 variants from organic winners, original audio. Welcome message pre-filled with a tracking keyword per ad: "الكتاب 1", "الكتاب 2", "الكتاب 3". Nadhem logs every sale with its keyword in a phone note.

## Metrics and thresholds (rough, Tunisia, small budget)
- **Hook rate** = 3-sec video plays ÷ impressions. Target >25%. Low → change the first 3 seconds.
- **Hold rate** = ThruPlays ÷ 3-sec plays. Target >10-15%. Low → body is slow.
- **CTR (link/all)** >1%. Low with good hook rate → offer/CTA unclear.
- **CPM**: Tunisia is cheap; if CPM jumps a lot, the creative is fatiguing or audience too narrow.
- **Cost per conversation** and **cost per sale** are the truth. Break-even for the book: cost per sale must stay well under 30 TND (aim ≤10 TND).
- **Close rate** = sales ÷ conversations. Low close rate is a DM-script problem, not an ad problem.

## Decision rules
- Kill an ad after ~$10 (≈30 TND) spent with no sale, or after 48h with hook rate <15%.
- Keep: cost per sale under target. Scale +20% budget every 2-3 days, never doubling overnight.
- Fatigue (frequency >3, CPM up, results down): new hook on the same body first, then a new angle.
- Every week: 1-2 new creatives enter the test, losers off. Keep a simple log: date | ad | spend | conversations | sales | cost per sale | note.

## Reading results
If Windsor (Meta Ads / Instagram) is connected, pull the numbers yourself; otherwise ask Nadhem for a screenshot of Ads Manager columns: spend, impressions, 3-sec plays, ThruPlays, CTR, messaging conversations, cost per conversation. Answer with: what worked, what to kill, the ONE next action. Never run write actions on ads without Nadhem confirming the exact change.

## Policy & safety
- No personal-attribute phrasing ("راك مدمن…؟"), no explicit wording/imagery, no medical guarantees, 18+ only. If rejected, rewrite the copy/first frame and request review once.
- Never advise spending money Nadhem doesn't have; ads come from profit or a fixed tiny test budget. A sale earned beats a big reach number.
