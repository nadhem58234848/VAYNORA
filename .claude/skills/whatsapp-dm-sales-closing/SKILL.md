# Closing sales in DM (غلق البيعة في الواتساب والانستغرام)

- HARD RULE: never message cold/unsaved numbers in bulk. WhatsApp restricted Nadhem once for that. Buyers message first (from reels, ads, bio link); follow-ups only to people who already wrote.
- The topic is private and shameful for the buyer. Discretion and zero judgment sell more than pressure.

## Principles
1. Reply fast (minutes, not hours). Speed is the biggest closing factor.
2. Talk like a brother who went through it, not like a shop. Short messages, voice notes are allowed and build trust.
3. Ask before you pitch: one question to understand him, then the offer that fits.
4. One clear next step per message (pay / send screenshot / join group).
5. Never lie about results, never promise a cure, never pressure with fake scarcity. Real deadlines only (e.g. challenge starts 1 Nov).
6. Track every conversation: keyword he came with ("الكتاب 1/2/3" = which ad), date, outcome. This feeds the ads skill.

## The flow (book example)
1. **Welcome + question** (he wrote "الكتاب"):
   "مرحبا بيك خويا 🤍 قبل كل شي، قداش عندك تعاني منها؟ وما تخافش، الكلام هذا يبقى بيناتنا."
2. **Mirror + hope** (based on his answer): "أنا زادة عشتها سنين، ونعرف الإحساس هاذا. الخبر الباهي اللي فما طريق."
3. **Offer, concrete**: what's inside (why you fall, the 6 triggers, the 10-minute rule, the 90-day challenge, answers to his questions), price 30 TND, how it arrives (PDF in minutes).
4. **Payment step**: exact instructions + "ابعثلي سكرين ونبعثلك الكتاب في الحين".
5. **Deliver + after-care**: send PDF, tell him where to start (which chapter tonight), invite to the free challenge group. A happy reader = testimonial + referral.

## Objections (answer with empathy, then one step)
- "غالي / ماعنديش فلوس": compare with what it costs him now; offer the free sample or free challenge first, no guilt. Never discount below the price Nadhem set without his OK.
- "نخمم / مبعد": "مافي حتى مشكل. برك خوذ الفصل الأول مجانا، كان نفعك كمّل." Then ONE follow-up after 24-48h, max two total.
- "جربت برشا حاجات وما نفعش": validate, explain what's different (plan + daily system + community, not just motivation).
- "مانجمش نخلص بـ Flouci": offer the other methods Nadhem actually accepts (check memory), never ask for card numbers in chat.
- "هل فما حد باش يعرف؟": guarantee privacy; PDF only, nothing published.
- Minor (under 18) or crisis signs (self-harm talk): no sale. Kind words, advise trusted adult / professional help; give him free content.

## Follow-up rules
- Only people who messaged first. Max 2 follow-ups, 24-48h apart, each adding value (a tip, a short voice note), not just "شبيك؟".
- Use WhatsApp Business labels: جديد / يستنا في الخلاص / شرى / ما شراش. Use Quick Replies ("/كتاب", "/خلاص") for the saved texts.

## Deliverable
When asked, give: ready-to-paste messages in darija (short, with emoji used sparingly), the quick-replies list, and an objection sheet. If Nadhem shares a real conversation, rewrite the next message for him and say why in one line.
