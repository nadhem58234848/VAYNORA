# Retention editing + ad creatives that sell (مونتاج يشد + كرييتيف يبيع)

## Constraints
- Nadhem has a phone only, zero budget. Free tools: CapCut mobile (free features only), free ChatGPT for images, Claude for scripts/text/edits. When Claude edits in the cloud, follow the `vaynora-edit` skill (Remotion, captions, loudnorm).
- Brand look: background #0D0D0D, red #C81E1E / #E0262B, Cairo font, handle @nadhem_elaashek. Captions: no card, Cairo ExtraBold 800, white with black outline, 2-3 words per line, keyword in red. Voice finished at -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP.
- Audio: original voice or royalty-free only. Licensed nasheeds block boosting/ads. No instruments (Nadhem's preference).

## Retention editing rules
1. **First frame = hook frame.** No logo intro, no black frame, no "السلام عليكم". Start on movement or the face mid-sentence, with hook text on screen from frame 1 (top-middle safe zone, not under the IG UI at the bottom 20%).
2. **Cut every silence and breath.** Jump cuts every 2-4s; alternate punch-in (110-120% zoom) on key words so the frame keeps changing.
3. **Visual change every 2-3 seconds**: zoom, B-roll, text pop, angle change. Never 5s of the same static shot.
4. **B-roll that illustrates the literal word** (prayer mat, phone at night, gym, Quran, walking, book cover, app screens). Nadhem wants motion graphics + B-roll in every edit.
5. **Captions always** (most watch muted). 2-3 words, synced, keyword red.
6. **Sound design light**: whoosh on transitions, low hit on the key line, silence before the payoff. Background nasheed/ambient at -25 dB under voice max.
7. **Re-hook at the drop-off point** (~40-50% of length): text like "والأهم…" or a visual shift.
8. **Ending**: cut right after the last word; or loop into the first frame. No 3-second outro.
9. **Length**: as short as the idea allows. 20-40s for tips, up to 90s for strong story episodes.
CapCut phone recipe to give Nadhem: Auto captions → style (white, black stroke, bold) → highlight keyword red manually; "Remove silences" / split + delete; Keyframe zoom 100→115%; Adjust volume + "Enhance voice"; export 1080p 30fps.

## Reading the result (tell Nadhem what to look at in IG insights)
- Skip rate / 3-sec views: if most leave in 3s, the hook failed → rewrite hook, keep body.
- Average watch time vs length: under 40% → body too slow, cut more.
- Sends & saves per reach: high = topic is gold, make a part 2 and consider it for ads.

## Ad creatives that sell
Rule #1: only advertise creatives already proven organically (high sends/saves, good avg watch). Boost/ads amplify, they don't fix.
Rule #2: the creative must match the product topic (porn-addiction reel → book «معركة الخلوات»; discipline/routine → challenge/app; gym/identity → VAYNORA shirt). Viral general faith reels bring the wrong buyers.

Ad creative formula (15-40s, 9:16):
1. Hook (0-3s): pain mirror or his question (from العميل-المثالي.md).
2. Agitate (3-10s): what it costs him (prayer, energy, self-respect, marriage hopes).
3. Proof (10-20s): Nadhem's story / numbers / a real message from a reader (with permission, no names) / book pages.
4. Offer (20-30s): what exactly he gets, price (book 30 TND), what makes it easy (PDF on WhatsApp in minutes).
5. CTA: one action, matched to the ad button ("ابعثلي «الكتاب 1» في الواتساب").
Make 3-5 variants that differ in the HOOK and ANGLE (hope / pain / contrarian / story / proof), not just colors: under Meta's Andromeda system creative diversity is what finds the audience.
Formats to rotate: talking head (best for trust), story-with-B-roll, text-on-screen + voice, carousel (book pages / 7 steps of falling), static image with strong headline (ChatGPT image + Claude overlays Arabic text, e.g. with video/src/Brand/VayCarousel.tsx).

Ad copy (primary text): line 1 = hook; 2-3 short lines of benefit; price/offer; CTA. Headline ≤ 6 words. Darija.

## Meta policy traps (avoid rejection)
- Never assert a personal attribute: NOT "راك مدمن على الإباحية؟". Use "برشا شباب يعانيو من…" / "كان تحس اللي…" / first-person story.
- No explicit words or imagery in the ad itself; say "العادة", "الإدمان", "معركة الخلوات". Don't show bodies, beds-at-night suggestive scenes.
- No guaranteed cures or medical claims; no before/after shaming.
- Target 18+ only (27% of followers are under 18: never target them).

## Deliverable format
For an edit: shot list with timestamps, caption text per beat, B-roll per beat, SFX cues, CapCut steps if Nadhem edits himself.
For ads: table of 3-5 variants: angle | hook (spoken + text) | creative source (which organic reel / new shoot) | primary text | headline | CTA keyword for tracking.
