# VAY carousel — «عندما تترك الإباحية تعود إليك 5 أشياء»

Arabic remake of the "Quit Porn → Energy Back" carousel with VAY.
ChatGPT draws the images (no text). Claude adds the Arabic text in Cairo and
assembles the 1080×1440 slides (two stacked panels, red divider, dark caption
boxes, vertical VAYNORA, counter pill). 7 slides, 12 images.

Gold/yellow from the original is replaced by red: gold belongs to AURA only.

3. Backgrounds must be CLEAN, TIDY and ORGANIZED: believable real places, minimal clutter, simple composition, plenty of breathing room. NOT oversaturated. Use a muted, natural palette: charcoal, warm grey, beige, soft wood, off-white. Red appears ONLY on VAY's own details (crown, chest emblem, hand wraps). No red smoke, no red glow, no red lightning, no red light sources, no fire.

4. Lighting tells the story: "BEFORE" images are dim, cool and quiet (evening, low light). "AFTER" images are bright with soft warm natural daylight.

5. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT anywhere: no letters, words, numbers, signs, posters with writing, screens with readable text, or watermarks. I will add the Arabic text myself.

6. Keep the bottom third of every image calm and simple (floor, table surface, wall) so text can be placed there. Keep VAY in the upper two thirds.

7. Format: landscape 3:2 (1536×1024) unless I say "portrait".

Reply "ready" and wait for my first image request.
